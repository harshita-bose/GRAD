About

CanSat is a design challenge in which student teams build a satellite the size of a soft-drink can, organised internationally by the American Astronautical Society and locally by ISRO. I worked on the team's descent, stabilisation and navigation.

What I did

  • Descent: contributed to the system that slows and controls the CanSat after release.
  • Stabilisation and navigation: worked on keeping the payload stable and tracking its position during the flight.
