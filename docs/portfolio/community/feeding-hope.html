About

During the COVID-19 lockdowns in 2021, I joined Feeding Hope, an initiative started by two friends to support families in Delhi whose livelihoods had been affected by the pandemic.

I took on the role of writer, creating Instagram content to raise awareness and mobilise support. Alongside the other volunteers, we sourced affordable ration supplies through a local supermarket and verified leads for assistance. Our outreach helped bring in donors and volunteers, enabling the initiative to support approximately 50 families, reaching nearly 150 individuals.

What I did

  • Created written content and Instagram posts for fundraising and awareness.
  • Helped mobilise donors and volunteers through social media outreach.
  • Participated in an initiative that coordinated ration support for approximately 50 families in Delhi.

What I took away

The experience introduced me to grassroots community mobilisation and showed me how communication and collective effort could translate into tangible support for families in need.
