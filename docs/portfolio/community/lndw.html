About

For Berlin’s Long Night of the Sciences (Lange Nacht der Wissenschaften) in June 2026, I joined my research group at IQP, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, in running a hands-on activity called “How to Build a Spectrometer.”

What I did

  • Hands-on building: visitors, many of them families with young children, built their own simple spectrometers.
  • Explaining the science: as they worked, I showed them how the instrument splits light into its individual colours and used this to introduce the basic ideas behind spectroscopy.

What I took away

The event helped bring scientific ideas to a broad audience, especially children. It also showed me how valuable it is to share research with people outside the lab.
