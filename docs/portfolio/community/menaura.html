About

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I co-founded Menaura with two teammates to develop a concept for making mental-health support more accessible and reducing the stigma surrounding mental-health conditions.

We developed Menaura as a proposed platform connecting individuals with qualified mental-health professionals, peer-support communities, and affordable support resources. Our concept also explored interactive educational resources, specialised exercises, counselling sessions, and tools for tracking individual progress.

We developed the venture and its proposed business model as part of our participation in the Diamond Challenge, an international student entrepreneurship competition.

What I did

  • Co-founded the Menaura team and contributed to developing the venture concept.
  • Worked on identifying barriers to mental-health support, particularly stigma, accessibility, and affordability.
  • Contributed to developing the proposed platform, its services, and its business model.
  • Collaborated with teammates to prepare the competition pitch deck.
